ABSTRACT: MediBot - Multilingual AI-Powered Medical Diagnostic System with Local Machine Learning

Advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Natural Language Processing (NLP) have revolutionized healthcare by enabling intelligent diagnostic systems that improve accessibility and accuracy of medical assessments. This work presents MediBot, a hybrid multilingual AI-based medical diagnostic platform that combines local machine learning classifiers with cloud-based language models to deliver efficient, privacy-preserving disease prediction and personalized health guidance. The system accepts diverse patient inputs—including demographic data, vital signs, medical history, and symptom descriptions—and employs Support Vector Machines (SVC) and Random Forest classifiers trained on real-world clinical datasets to predict the likelihood of diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and anemia. Beyond classical prediction, MediBot integrates Google Gemini API for conversational medical advice, symptom analysis, and comprehensive health recommendations including personalized diet and exercise regimens. The platform further enhances utility by intelligently locating nearby hospitals and specialized medical practitioners based on user geolocation, with multilingual response delivery supporting inclusivity across diverse populations. By leveraging local machine learning for rapid, privacy-aware classical disease prediction while maintaining cloud-based NLP for rich contextual understanding, dynamic form generation based on dataset columns, and geolocation-based service discovery, MediBot addresses critical gaps in existing systems: computational efficiency through local inference, data privacy preservation, elimination of language barriers, lack of personalization, and inadequate clinical decision support. The modular architecture enables seamless integration of additional disease models and specialist recommendations, making it a scalable solution for accessible, intelligent healthcare delivery.

Keywords: Machine Learning, Disease Prediction, Multilingual Healthcare Chatbot, SVC/Random Forest Classification, Local ML Inference, Conversational AI, Geolocation Services, Medical NLP, Privacy-Preserving Healthcare
